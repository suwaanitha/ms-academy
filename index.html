Let’s have a conversation ↗

Which grades can join MS Academy?

Classes are available for students in Grades 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10. Contact Teacher Suwaanitha to discuss subjects and availability for your grade.

Are all classes taught in English?

All subjects are taught in English medium. Religion also includes bilingual support.

Can my child attend classes online?

Yes. Both online and physical classes are available. Contact Teacher Suwaanitha to discuss your preferred class format.

When do classes start?

The announced start date is 14 September onwards. Days and times are arranged according to the grades. Please contact us to confirm your current timetable.

What does it cost to join?

The admission fee is Rs. 500. Please contact Teacher Suwaanitha on 075 153 5938 for subject tuition fees and payment details.

Where are physical classes held?

Contact Teacher Suwaanitha on 075 153 5938 to confirm the physical class venue and availability before attending.
